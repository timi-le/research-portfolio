## Research manuscripts &#40;single author&#41;

### 1\. Adaptive Participation and Market Feedback: A Deployment Audit and Reproducible Simulation Study

**Timilehin Olapade &#40;2026&#41;\. Manuscript submitted to AISTATS 2027; under review, not accepted\.**

Operational audit of 28,165 logged events, examination of four exported model output variants over 5,998 common inputs and 1,312 controlled synthetic feedback episodes\. Studies participation and shock interactions while explicitly separating observed model agreement from predictive validity and modeled mechanisms from live market causality\.

[Manuscript PDF](https://github.com/timi-le/Adaptive-AI-Market-Transitions/blob/main/papers/market/main.pdf) \| [Research code and reproducibility](https://github.com/timi-le/Adaptive-AI-Market-Transitions)

### 2\. Market Transitions under AI Financial Models: Alpha Decay, Instability, and Evidence\-Gated Neuro\-Symbolic Adaptation

**Timilehin Olapade &#40;31 October 2026&#41;\. Public working paper; not peer reviewed\.**

Explores interaction among learning systems and market states using structural estimation diagnostics and a simulated environment containing 24 adaptive participants across 256 parameter configurations with 64 replications each\. Evaluates leverage, liquidity, model similarity, evidence requirements, adaptation speed and synthetic instability\. Distinguishes improved forecasts from simply reducing participation or losses\.

[Working paper, reproducible code and results](https://github.com/timi-le/Market-Transitions-under-AI-Financial-Models)

### 3\. Agentic Causal Macro Intelligence: An Identification\-Aware Framework for Regime\-Conditional Expert Routing

**Timilehin Olapade &#40;31 October 2026 revision&#41;\. Public working paper; not peer reviewed\.**

Investigates evidence constrained expert routing under uncertain market regimes\. Combines causal hypothesis checks, identification safeguards, expert eligibility, model selection and abstention\. Reproducible diagnostics generate 800 synthetic datasets and 4,000 method results\. Some experimental material overlaps manuscript 2 and is not presented as independent evidence\.

[Revised manuscript and code](https://github.com/timi-le/An-Identification-Aware-Framework-for-Regime-Conditional-Expert-Routing) \| [Original Zenodo preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21561542) \| [ResearchGate publication page](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/413861316_Agentic_Causal_Macro_Intelligence_A_Mixture-of-Experts_Framework_for_Regime-Conditional_Systematic_Trading)
